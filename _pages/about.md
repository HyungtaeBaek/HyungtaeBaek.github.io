I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics at Jeonbuk National University, Jeonju, Korea.
I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Kyungpook National University in August 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Jung Wook Lim.
My doctoral thesis was titled *A quotient extension of the polynomial ring: The Anderson ring*.

My research lies in commutative algebra, mainly **multiplicative ideal theory**.
I study star-operations (especially the $w$-operation), $S$-Noetherian-type conditions, and ring constructions such as Anderson rings, Nagata rings and Serre's conjecture rings.
More recently, I have also been working on **representation theory**, including modules over nil-DAHAs, and on **homological algebra**.

Research interests
------
* Multiplicative ideal theory
* Representation theory
* Homological algebra
