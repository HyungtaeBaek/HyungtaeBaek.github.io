I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics at Jeonbuk National University, Jeonju, Korea.
I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Kyungpook National University in August 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Jung Wook Lim,
with the thesis *A quotient extension of the polynomial ring: The Anderson ring*.
Before that, I received my M.S. in Mathematics from Kyungpook National University (2022) and my B.S. in Mathematics Education from Seowon University (2020).

My research lies in commutative algebra, mainly **multiplicative ideal theory**, and more recently also in **representation theory**.

Research
------

**Anderson rings.**
For a commutative ring $R$, the *Anderson ring* of $R$ is the quotient ring $R[X]_A$ of the polynomial ring, where $A=\\{f\in R[X] \mid f(0)=1\\}$.
It is a common subring of the Nagata ring and the Serre's conjecture ring (up to isomorphism).
A central theme of my work is to understand how ring-theoretic properties transfer between $R$ and $R[X]_A$,
including Prüfer-like and Krull-like conditions, star-operations, and the $w$-maximal spectrum of $R[X]_A$.

**Star-operations and $w$-Hilbert domains.**
I study integral domains through the $w$-operation.
I introduced the notion of a *$w$-Hilbert domain*, studied its relationship with Hilbert domains, strong Mori domains and UMT domains,
and characterized when polynomial rings and Anderson rings are $w$-Hilbert domains.
I have also worked on characterizations of $t$-almost Dedekind domains via $w$-secondary modules,
and on local properties of integral domains under extensions and pullback constructions.

**$S$-Noetherian-type conditions.**
Given a multiplicative subset $S$ of a ring, I study "$S$-versions" of classical notions:
$S$-Noetherian modules and $S$-strong Mori modules (including a Hilbert basis theorem for them),
and $S$-$n$-absorbing ideals, a common generalization of $S$-prime ideals and $n$-absorbing ideals.

**Representation theory.**
More recently, I have been working on representations of a nil-DAHA of type $(C_1^{\vee},C_1)$,
classifying its finite-dimensional irreducible multiplicity-free modules.

See [Papers](/papers/) for my publications and preprints, and [Talks](/talks/) for my presentations.
