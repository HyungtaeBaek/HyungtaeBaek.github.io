My research lies in commutative algebra, mainly **multiplicative ideal theory**, and more recently in **representation theory**.
Below is an overview of the main topics, with related papers and talks.

Anderson rings
======
Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity and $X$ an indeterminate over $R$.
The *Anderson ring* of $R$ is the quotient ring $R[X]_A$ of the polynomial ring $R[X]$, where
$A=\\{f\in R[X] \mid f(0)=1\\}$.
It is a common subring of the Nagata ring and the Serre's conjecture ring (up to isomorphism),
and it was the subject of my Ph.D. thesis, *A quotient extension of the polynomial ring: The Anderson ring*.

I study how ring-theoretic properties transfer between $R$ and $R[X]_A$:
Prüfer-like and Krull-like conditions, star-operations on $R[X]_A$, and the structure of its $w$-maximal spectrum.

Star-operations and $w$-Hilbert domains
======
Star-operations, and in particular the $w$-operation, give a flexible framework to extend classical notions of integral domains.
I introduced the notion of a *$w$-Hilbert domain*, studied its relationship with Hilbert domains, strong Mori domains and UMT domains
(with examples from $D+M$ constructions), and characterized when polynomial rings and Anderson rings are $w$-Hilbert domains.
I have also worked on $t$-almost Dedekind domains via $w$-secondary modules,
and on how local properties of integral domains behave under extensions and pullback constructions.

Representation theory
======
More recently, I have been working on the representation theory of a nil-DAHA of type $(C_1^{\vee},C_1)$.
We classify its finite-dimensional irreducible modules that are multiplicity-free with respect to two natural operators
and admit an adapted block basis, by constructing two explicit families of modules.
